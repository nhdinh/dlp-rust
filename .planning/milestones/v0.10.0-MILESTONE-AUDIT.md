## Summary

The v0.10.0 milestone **cannot be completed as-shipped**. The three critical real-time blocking pipeline blockers identified in the June 21 audit have been resolved:

1. `HookIpcServer` is now constructed in `dlp-agent/src/service.rs` with `bypass_tx` wired.
2. The `bypass_tx` sender is no longer discarded; BypassAlerts route through the correlator to SIEM.
3. `VolumeClassQuery` is wired from hook DLL through the named pipe to `VolumeDetector`.

The remaining blockers are confined to **Phase 58 Differentiators**, whose implementation files were committed and then reverted from HEAD:

- **DIFF-02**: Diagnostic dashboard files absent.
- **DIFF-03**: `content_sha256` evidence column absent.
- **DIFF-04**: Self-health telemetry dashboard files absent.

Additionally, **BLOCK-02** remains partial (CopyFile2 indirect coverage only) and **DIFF-01** remains partially wired (approval cache consulted but no full E2E grant flow).
