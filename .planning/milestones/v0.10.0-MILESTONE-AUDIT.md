## Summary

The v0.10.0 milestone **cannot be completed as-shipped**. Eight of sixteen phases have no `VERIFICATION.md`, two phases have no `VALIDATION.md`, and the integration checker found **critical blockers** in the core real-time blocking pipeline:

1. `HookIpcServer` is never constructed in `dlp-agent/src/service.rs`, so hook DLLs have no named-pipe server to talk to.
2. The `bypass_tx` sender for ntdll/ETW BypassAlerts is discarded, breaking bypass-alert routing.
3. Phase 58 differentiator files (`diagnostic_store.rs`, `diagnostic_aggregator.rs`, `health_aggregator.rs`, `content_sha256` schema) were committed and then reverted from HEAD, breaking DIFF-02, DIFF-03, and DIFF-04.
