## Cross-Phase Integration Findings

### Critical Blockers

1. **Hook journal never written (ETW-02 / ETW-03)**
   - The hook journal module exists in `dlp-hook-dll/src/hook_journal.rs` and the agent's `BypassCorrelator` reads from `Global\DlpHookJournal_<pid>`.
   - **No trampoline calls `journal_write()`**. The ETW bypass correlator will always see `NoHookJournal`, producing false-positive bypass alerts or missing real bypasses.

2. **Hook-derived BypassAlert may not be consumed (ETW-03 / ETW-05)**
   - `IpcPayloadV1::BypassAlert` exists and the agent's `handle_connection` routes bypass payloads to `bypass_tx`.
   - `bypass_rx` is passed to `BypassCorrelator::run()`, but the correlator's main loop does not visibly poll this receiver; hook-derived alerts (EDR detection, hook overwrite) may be dropped.

3. **UAT not executed (OPS-04)**
   - Phase 57 VERIFICATION.md lists OPS-04 as PENDING. All 36 UAT scenarios are NOT EXECUTED and CRIT-04 benchmarks are TBD. The ship/no-ship decision is blocked.

### Warnings

- **CACHE-03**: `dlp-agent/src/hook_ipc.rs::build_cache_hint()` uses path-text heuristics rather than real PolicyStore classification.
- **DACL-04**: Two-phase staged update server->agent push protocol is not clearly traceable.
- **DIFF-02 / DIFF-03 / DIFF-04**: `diagnostic_ring.rs`, `hash_compute.rs`, and health snapshot modules are declared in the hook DLL but never invoked from trampolines.
