# Plan Review Convergence — Phase 58.5

## Convergence Outcome

The cross-AI plan-review-convergence workflow ran for three cycles. The final external review (Cycle 3, Codex gpt-5.5) found **no unresolved HIGH concerns** and **four actionable MEDIUM concerns**.

Because the workflow reached its maximum cycle budget, convergence stops here. The four MEDIUM concerns are recorded below as **accepted residual risks** to be tightened during implementation or addressed in a follow-up planning cycle before execution if desired.

## What Changed Across Cycles

- **Cycle 1** identified blocking IPC-topology, shared-memory lifetime, registry-access, and watchdog-audit issues. Plans were revised to introduce a DLL-polling protocol (`PollControl`/`ControlResponse`), active-call guards, lock-protected `Option<Mapping>` globals, and service-visible watchdog evidence.
- **Cycle 2** verified the polling architecture but raised four remaining HIGH concerns about control-thread startup, unload safety, `OnceLock` reset mechanics, and `UnhookAck` success handling. Plans were revised again (commit `2b1c100`) to start the control thread from a safe post-attach/init path, add active-call drain semantics, replace `OnceLock` with `Mutex<Option<Mapping>>`, and only transition registry entries to `Exited` on `ack.success == true`.
- **Cycle 3** confirmed all prior HIGH concerns resolved but surfaced four MEDIUM concerns around local spoofing, shutdown budget proof, shared-memory reader lifetime, and watchdog evidence integrity.

## Residual MEDIUM Risks (Accepted for Implementation)

| ID | Concern | Location | Recommended Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEDIUM-1 | Control-plane frames can be spoofed by any Authenticated Users process because the hook pipe grants AU read/write and `PollControl`/`UnhookAck` are validated only by `pid + creation_time`. | `dlp-agent/src/ipc/pipe_security.rs:15-23`, `58.5-03-PLAN.md:123-161` | Add a per-injection session token/nonce to `PollControl`, `UnhookCommand`, and `UnhookAck`; validate the token against the registry entry before returning a command or accepting an ack. Consider a tighter DACL or a separate control pipe. |
| MEDIUM-2 | The 5 s `UNHOOK_WAIT_BUDGET` is not proven to fit inside the existing 10 s service shutdown timeout alongside other cleanup waits. | `dlp-agent/src/service.rs:84-85`, `58.5-03-PLAN.md:212-219` | Compute a remaining-deadline budget at shutdown start and clamp the unhook budget to `min(5s, deadline - safety_margin)`. Add an explicit test that exercises worst-case existing cleanup plus unhook. |
| MEDIUM-3 | Shared-memory readers may use raw mapped pointers after the mapping global is cleared. | `58.5-02-PLAN.md:135-147`, `dlp-hook-dll/src/trampolines.rs:174-223` | Require readers to hold a read guard or an `Arc`-owned mapping clone for the duration of pointer use, or prove every reader is covered by `enter_trampoline`/`leave_trampoline`. |
| MEDIUM-4 | Watchdog evidence files under ProgramData are writable by local users, allowing tampering or audit spoofing. | `58.5-02-PLAN.md:267`, `58.5-03-PLAN.md:201-208` | Include `creation_time` in the filename, use create-new semantics, restrict ACLs to SYSTEM/Administrators, and validate evidence with a per-injection nonce or HMAC before transitioning registry state. |
