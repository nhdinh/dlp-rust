## Objective

Verify that `source_volume_class` and `destination_volume_class` fields exist on `HookRequest` and `EvaluateRequest`, that the `From<EvaluateRequest> for AbacContext` conversion forwards these fields, and that backward-compatibility tests prove old peers deserialize correctly. This plan is a verification pass — the fields were added during Phase 56 execution and must be confirmed present before Plan 02 and Plan 03 proceed.

Purpose: Without these fields on `HookRequest`, the hook DLL cannot communicate volume class information across the IPC boundary. Without them on `EvaluateRequest`, the REST API cannot carry volume class to the Policy Engine. Without forwarding in the `From` impl, `AbacContext` always has `None` for volume classes regardless of what the request carries.

Output: Verified `HookRequest`, `EvaluateRequest`, and `AbacContext` conversion with full backward-compatibility tests.
