## Review feedback addressed in this plan

- **ApprovalUse vs ApprovalOverride (MEDIUM, both reviewers):** `ApprovalUse` (Phase 61) was intended for "token consumed at enforcement time" but was never emitted. `ApprovalOverride` (this phase) is the enforcement-time event that supersedes it. Doc comments on both variants will clarify the distinction. `ApprovalUse` is NOT removed to avoid breaking existing SIEM rules that may reference it; it is documented as reserved/unused.
