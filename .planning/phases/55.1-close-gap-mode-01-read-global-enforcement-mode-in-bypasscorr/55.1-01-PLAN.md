<objective>
Add `enforcement_mode: EnforcementMode` to `CorrelatorConfig` and `BypassCorrelator`, then wire `agent_config.enforcement.global_mode` through `service.rs` at correlator construction time. This is the structural prerequisite for Audit-mode suppression logic.

Purpose: Without this wiring, the correlator cannot know the global enforcement mode and will continue emitting false-positive bypass alerts in Audit mode (MODE-01 gap per v0.10.0 milestone audit INT-WARN-02).
Output: `CorrelatorConfig` and `BypassCorrelator` carry the mode; `service.rs` passes it from `AgentConfig`.
</objective>
