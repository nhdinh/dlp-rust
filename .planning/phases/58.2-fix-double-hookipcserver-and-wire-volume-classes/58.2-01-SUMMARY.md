## Decisions Made

- Consolidated all construction into single spawn_hook_ipc_server call to eliminate the named-pipe race condition (T-58.2-01)
- Chained all three .with_* handlers on the consolidated server to prevent loss of DIFF-01/02/04 functionality (T-58.2-02)
- Stored the single handle in RunLoopContext::hook_ipc_handle only, removing BlockingThreads::hook_ipc to prevent double-join (T-58.2-03)
- Made hook_request_to_evaluate_request take resolved caller_sid as parameter for pure, testable conversion
- Mapped MOVE/RENAME/REPLACE/SET_INFO/NT_SET_INFO all to Action::DELETE per integration test expectations and established convention
