## Objective

Close the DEVICE-05 and TAMPER-04 cross-cutting gap by replacing all hardcoded `DeviceHealthStatus::default()` (which is `Healthy`) in agent ABAC subject construction with the live `current_health()` value. This ensures ABAC `DeviceHealth` policy conditions evaluate against the real endpoint health state — including `Tampered` after a hash-chain break is detected.

## Scope Boundary

- In scope: Three hardcoded `device_health` sites in `identity.rs` and `interception/mod.rs`.
- Out of scope: Server-side changes (Plan 01), TUI screen (Plan 03), new event types, new health states.
