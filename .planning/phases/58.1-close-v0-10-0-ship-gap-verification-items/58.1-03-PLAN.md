## Threat Model

| Threat | STRIDE | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Fabricated verification evidence | Tampering | D-10: evidence must be traceable to test names, commit SHAs, or log excerpts; "VERIFIED BY INSPECTION" is acceptable with explicit rationale |

**Blocking threshold:** None — documentation gaps are not high-severity security threats, but they block the milestone ship gate per D-11.
