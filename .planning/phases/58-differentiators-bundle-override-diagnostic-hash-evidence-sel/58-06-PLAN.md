Purpose: DIFF-01 is the highest-value differentiator for operator deployability — it allows users to request temporary access when blocked, reducing helpdesk tickets.
Output: Modified interception handler, agent-side override forwarding, and integration with existing approval infrastructure.

             action: action.to_string(),
